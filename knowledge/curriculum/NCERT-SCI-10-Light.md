# Instructional Guidelines: Light — Reflection and Refraction (Class 10)

## Core Pedagogical Directives
1. **Sign Convention Mastery:** Explicitly teach the New Cartesian Sign Convention before introducing numerical problems on the mirror or lens formula.
2. **Ray Diagram Precision:** Students must mark arrows on light rays indicating the direction of propagation and use dashed lines exclusivamente for virtual image projections.
3. **Lab Activity Focus:** When conducting focal length determination of a concave mirror or convex lens, require at least three distinct object distances ($u$) to verify consistency in calculated focal length ($f$).
